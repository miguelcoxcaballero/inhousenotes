# v5.16.14

Folder contents fade in over the folder itself.

- Opening a folder now crossfades its board colour and sheets into the interior background while the lid turns, instead of the interior popping in under the lid. Closing reverses the same fade before the folder lands on its card.
- Removes the flicker at both ends of the transition caused by that abrupt swap.
- Web only. The Android A catalog remains on the previous verified APK; no new native build is required.
