# Inhouse Notes v5.11.62

Visible bullet-guided straightening and restored scan templates.

While a photograph is processed, the review shows the bullet points actually detected on that photograph and the curved rows and columns they form. It then shows the real intermediate straightened photograph with the measured point positions and faint target guides. Missing observations remain missing. These diagnostic lines disappear before the finished scan is shown or added to a document.

The existing physical page model now accepts a small, smooth correction from measured bullet positions when independent held-out observations confirm better alignment. Displacement, local scale, folds and geometric acceptance remain bounded. A well-verified fit skips redundant alternative bend-axis searches; difficult pages retain the full fitting search.

After straightening and colour cleanup, verified scans receive the original printable template: the yellow frame, lower boxes, four colour markers and bullet pattern. The template shares its source with the downloadable stencil and preserves handwriting through transparent composition. Rejected photographs remain available as unchanged originals.

Detection avoids repeated calculations, and colour cleanup reuses verified bullet locations when the photographed dots confirm those positions. All three captured photographs continue to process in parallel. Their progress and previews stay attached to the correct thumbnail, completed results appear immediately, and the best verified scan is selected when the batch finishes.

Android 1.0.16 (versionCode 17) loads these scanner changes from the website. No APK reinstall or update catalog change is required. Physical Android camera performance has not been measured for this release.
