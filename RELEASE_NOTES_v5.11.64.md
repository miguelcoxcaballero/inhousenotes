# Inhouse Notes v5.11.64

Cleaner paper, darker writing and fewer duplicate bullets.

Scan correction checks photographed paper independently of the dots accepted by the geometric fit. Missing geometry observations no longer force the whole sheet through a less precise lighting correction. The local paper estimate follows abrupt shadows while retaining pen, pencil, colour and filled drawings.

Photographed dot cleanup accounts for blurred dot size and preserves strokes extending beyond the printed mark. The native editor template remains separate from the scanned ink and uses the same renderer, colours and opaque markers as other document pages.

Before replacing a photographed template, the scanner checks the actual corrected pixels for coherent interior misalignment. A frame with verified yellow edges but a failed interior correction keeps its original photograph instead of receiving a misleading straight grid.

Automatic burst selection compares the corrected paper as well as independently validated geometry. Missing quality measurements are treated as unknown, and choosing a thumbnail manually still keeps that choice.

Android 1.0.16 (versionCode 17) receives this update from the website. No APK reinstall or update catalog change is required. Desktop browser checks do not establish performance on a physical Android device.
