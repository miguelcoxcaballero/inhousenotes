# v6.1.0

Pen writing (pending the owner's approval of `docs/v6/BEHAVIOUR-CHANGES.md` §7).

- Pen ink follows the stylus pressure: thin when pressing lightly, thicker when pressing hard, with natural
  tapering. The same ink everywhere: on the page, in thumbnails, in other devices' live view and in exported PDFs.
  Strokes without varying pressure (mouse, touch, older notes) look exactly as before.
- Less lag: the live ink follows the browser's prediction of the pen tip for one frame (never stored).
- S Pen side button: hold it to erase with the chosen eraser; release it to write again. The toolbar tool does
  not change.
- Web assets updated; Android APK and frozen installer unchanged.
