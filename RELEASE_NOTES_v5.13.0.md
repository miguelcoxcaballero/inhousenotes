# v5.13.0

New home. A bottom bar switches between Home, Starred, Shared and Files, with the same size and style as the inhouse photos navigation. Recents stay at the top of Home.

Every notebook is shown as a full A4 page with its pages stacked underneath; favorites carry a yellow tab and, on a computer, hovering a page shows its quick actions. Folders are vertical manila folders: opening one plays a real opening that swings the cover open like a book and zooms into it until the screen takes the folder's colour. Closing reverses it.

The bin stays next to the search field and the + button keeps its original look, now just above the bottom bar on phones. This is a web only release; the Android app picks it up automatically.
