# v5.15.5

File names and menus inside thumbnails.

Web only; Android loads this update without a new APK.

- Home file cards place their name, date and actions over the bottom of the
  preview, using a blurred dark gradient and white text for contrast.
- Landscape previews use a compact single-line footer; missing or failed
  thumbnails retain their title and actions.
- Menus keep full touch targets and open outside the thumbnail without clipping.

Checked on desktop and mobile, in light and dark themes, with portrait,
landscape, narrow and unavailable previews. Offline library checks pass.
