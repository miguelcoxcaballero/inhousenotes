# v6.0.0

Inhouse Notes rewritten in Rust/WebAssembly: the same screens and behaviour as 5.19.0, with a new document model and instant sync.

- The app is one Rust program (`rust/`, compiled to `wasm/ihn.wasm`) behind a thin JavaScript host. The markup and styles are 5.18.0's, and a pixel-parity suite targets 5.19.0. Existing Drive documents open as before, and local 5.x data is migrated once on first start.
- Edits are operations that reach other devices at once over P2P, or through small sync files in Drive within about 4 s. The PDF in Drive is rebuilt by one device after 10 s of inactivity, when you leave, and on Export.
- Measured against 5.18.0 (desktop Chromium, Drive mock): Home shows its first card in 280 ms instead of 458 ms, with 31 requests / 2.2 MB instead of 47 / 3.9 MB. A 20-page 5.x notebook opens in 1.24 s instead of 1.65 s. After opening it the tab holds 12.5–13.2 MB instead of 31.2–31.4 MB (peak while opening: 65–75 MB instead of 92–103 MB). Eraser script time is 0.03 ms per move instead of 0.52 ms. Pen frames no longer stall (worst frame 16.8 ms instead of 66.6 ms). A whole edit session on a dense page uses 3.9–4.6 s of CPU instead of 4.2–5.0 s. Scrolling and zooming cost the same.
- Behaviour changes, **pending the owner's approval** (`docs/v6/BEHAVIOUR-CHANGES.md`): 5.x bugs fixed (selection, images and cross-page drag on non-A4 pages; single-tap dots kept; erase undo keeps the z-order; fast erasing no longer skips thin strokes; PDF page provenance and rotated or cropped PDF pages in exports; Night mode updates the status-bar colour). Exported ink is a vector layer, with no raster fallback. Only editors can connect or send changes. A `Inhouse Notes sync` folder appears in My Drive. Drive's "modified by" may name another collaborator. A still-open 5.x tab cannot open documents saved by 6.0.0 until it reloads. The service worker no longer uploads in the background. Uploads and new folders need a connection. Needs Android System WebView 96 or newer. pdf.js and pdf-lib load on first use, and jsPDF is gone.
- Known limits: the download is larger: `ihn.wasm` is 596 KB gzip (1.33 MB raw), against 456 KB gzip of first-party JS in 5.18.0. GitHub Pages serves gzip only. Images and version snapshots do not travel over P2P or the relay: they arrive with the next PDF export. Drive sync is verified only against the test mock, not yet against real Google accounts.
- The 5.x runtime stays deployed for tabs still open on 5.x; they reload into 6.0.0 at their next check. Those files go in 6.0.1.
- Google sign-in uses the same shared OAuth module as 5.19.0. Android APK 1.0.29 (versionCode 30, channel A) supports shared account discovery. Signing out applies only to Notes.
